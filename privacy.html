Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Cytecho collects through this website, what we do with it, and the rights you have over it. We have tried to keep it short and in plain English.

Who we are

Cytecho is a cybersecurity business based in Haverfordwest, Wales, United Kingdom. For the information described here, Cytecho is the “controller”, which means we decide how and why it is used.

You can reach us about anything in this policy at info@cytecho.net or on +44 7311 113990.

The short version

  • We only receive personal details when you choose to email, call or message us.
  • We use them to reply to you and to provide the help you asked for. We do not sell them.
  • This website has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

What we collect

WhenWhatWhy
You use the contact form or email us Your name, email address, and anything else you include, such as your company, phone number and message. To reply to your enquiry and keep a record of our conversation.
You use the “Under attack?” form Your name, organisation, email, phone number and the details of the incident you describe. To contact you quickly and understand what is happening.
You phone us Your phone number and what you tell us. To deal with your call and follow up if needed.
You visit the website Technical details that any website receives, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. To deliver the pages to you and keep the site secure.

How the forms work. The forms on this site do not send anything to our servers. When you press send, your own email app opens with a message addressed to us, and nothing reaches us until you send that email.

Please do not include passwords, card numbers or other secrets in a message. If we need sensitive information to help with an incident, we will agree a safer way to share it.

Our legal reasons for using it

  • Legitimate interests. Replying to enquiries, running and securing this website, and keeping business records.
  • Contract. Taking the steps you ask for before you become a client, and providing our services once you are one.
  • Consent. Any optional cookies, which stay off unless you accept them. See our cookie policy.
  • Legal obligation. Where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. It is handled by a small number of suppliers who help us run the business, and only as far as they need:

  • Our email provider, which stores the messages you send us.
  • Our website hosting provider, which receives the technical details described above when it serves a page.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typeface and receives your IP address to do so.

We may also share information where the law requires it, or with your agreement, for example with a specialist we bring in to help with your incident.

Transfers outside the UK

Some of these suppliers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as adequacy regulations or approved contract terms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as it takes to deal with them and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us. Information about work we do for clients is kept for as long as the law and our contracts require. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

How we protect it

We limit access to the information you send us to the people who need it to help you. No method of sending information over the internet is perfectly secure, so please share only what is needed.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, or limit how we use it;
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • send it to you or another organisation in a portable format;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time.

To use any of these rights, email info@cytecho.net. We will reply within one month and there is normally no charge.

If you are not happy

Please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Children

This website is aimed at businesses and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we collect or how we use it changes, and revise the date at the top.
