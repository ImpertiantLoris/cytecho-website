What to do right now

  1. Disconnect, do not switch off.Take affected devices off the network, but leave them powered on so evidence in memory is kept.
  2. Keep the evidence.Do not wipe, reinstall or delete anything yet. Save ransom notes, suspicious emails and logs.
  3. Change access from a clean device.Reset passwords for affected accounts from a machine you trust, and sign out other sessions.
  4. Write down what you know.What you saw, when it started and which systems are affected. It makes our first conversation much faster.
