This policy explains what this website stores on your device, why, and how you can change your mind. It covers cookies and similar technologies such as your browser's local storage.

The short version

  • We store one small item so the site can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies.
  • We do not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • If we add optional cookies in future, they will only be switched on after you press Accept.
  • You can change your choice at any time using the button above or the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.

What we store

NameTypePurposeKept for
cytecho-cookie-consent Strictly necessary (local storage) Remembers whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies, and the date, so we do not ask on every page. 6 months, then we ask again

Strictly necessary storage does not need your consent, because the site cannot respect your choice without it. It holds no personal details and is never sent to us.

Optional cookies

None are in use today. If we introduce any, for example to measure how many people visit a page, we will list each one here with its purpose and how long it lasts, and it will stay off unless you have accepted.

Other services this site connects to

  • Google Fonts. The typeface is loaded from Google's servers. To deliver it, Google receives your IP address and basic browser details. Google states that it does not set cookies for this.
  • Instagram. Our Instagram link takes you to instagram.com. Nothing from Instagram loads on this site, but once you follow the link their own cookie policy applies.
  • Your email app. The contact and incident forms open your own email app with a message addressed to us. The details you type are not stored on this site.

Changing or withdrawing your choice

Press “Change cookie settings” at the top of this page, or “Cookie settings” in the footer, and choose again. Rejecting is always as easy as accepting.

You can also clear or block cookies and site storage in your browser settings. If you do, the site still works, and you will simply be asked for your choice again.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page whenever what we store changes, and revise the date at the top.
